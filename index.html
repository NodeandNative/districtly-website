🌟 Best Time to Visit Wayanad
Peak (Nov–Feb): Crisp 18–22°C mornings, clear trails, zero rain. Wildlife safari season — best elephant and tiger sightings. Book stays 4+ weeks ahead. Prices 40% higher.

Shoulder (Mar–Apr, Sep–Oct): Warm but manageable. Coffee bloom in March is spectacular. Oct waterfalls are full post-monsoon. 25% fewer tourists. Best value month: October.

Avoid (May–Aug): Southwest monsoon brings 400mm+ monthly rainfall. Many trekking trails closed. Landslide risk on Kalpetta–Mananthavady road. Only for those who love dramatic misty forests.

🌧️ Wayanad Rainfall Insight
Wayanad receives one of the highest rainfall totals in India during the Southwest Monsoon (June–September). July peak exceeds 420mm in a single month — more than London's entire year. The Eastern slopes near Sulthan Bathery receive less rain than the Western slopes near Vythiri. Plan accordingly.

💡 Smart Traveller Insight
Best value window: October. Post-monsoon waterfalls are still full, temperatures drop to a pleasant 23°C, crowds are 60% lower than December, and hotel rates are 30–40% cheaper. The trails are washed clean. Coffee estates are a vivid green. This is Wayanad's best-kept secret month.
